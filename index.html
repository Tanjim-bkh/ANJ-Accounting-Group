Tax dates through the year

The Australian financial year runs from 1 July to 30 June. These are the dates the ATO holds most people and businesses to.

  • Financial year begins1 July
  • Single Touch Payroll finalisation14 July
  • Monthly GST and PAYG withholdingLodgement and payment21st of each month
  • Quarterly BASLodgement and payment28 Oct, Feb, Apr, Jul
  • Super guarantee contributionsFor each quarter28 Oct, Jan, Apr, Jul
  • Tax return, if you lodge it yourselfWith a registered tax agent the date can be later31 October
  • End of the financial year30 June
